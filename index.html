Connect to your truck

  1. Plug the OBD adapter into the port under the dash on the driver's side.
  2. Turn the ignition on, engine off.
  3. Press Connect and pick the adapter from the list.

OBDLink MX+: pair it once in your phone's or computer's Bluetooth settings (press the button on the adapter to make it visible), then use the first button and pick it from the list. Android needs Chrome 137 or newer.

This browser cannot reach an OBD adapter, so only the demo truck is available. Use Chrome on Android, or Chrome or Edge on a computer. iPhone and iPad browsers do not allow it.

Connection options 115200 suits OBDLink adapters; many generic ELM327 adapters use 38400.
